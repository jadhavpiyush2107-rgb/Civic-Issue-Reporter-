Connect with Shirpur Municipal Council

Shirpur Municipal Council plays an important role in maintaining and improving essential civic services across the city. Through Civic Issue Reporter, citizens can easily report problems such as potholes, damaged roads, streetlight issues, garbage, drainage problems, and other public concerns, helping authorities identify and address issues more efficiently.

ABOUT US

We are a team of passionate Computer Science Engineering students who came from R C Patel Institue Of Technology to develop Civic Issue Reporter as a practical solution for the people of Shirpur. With our interest in technology and problem-solving, we designed this platform to make reporting civic issues easier, faster, and more accessible. This project represents our teamwork, creativity, and effort to use technology for creating a positive impact in our community.
